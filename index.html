An Official-Looking Petition

We, the Undersigned (Clickers),
Demand 700 Pages.

Of what? Unclear. Why 700? Also unclear. But the number feels right, and this petition collects absolutely no information about you — not your name, not your email, not even your opinion. Just your click.

Whereas:

  • 700 pages is a nice, round-ish, oddly specific number;
  • Nobody asked for this, and yet here we are;
  • Every great movement starts with an unnecessary website;
  • The button below is very satisfying to press.

Therefore, we resolve to:

  • Click a button;
  • Watch a number go up;
  • Feel a small, fleeting sense of civic accomplishment;
  • Tell absolutely no one, or everyone, your choice.

This petition is entirely for fun. No names, emails, IP addresses, or personal information are collected or stored — we only keep a single shared number that goes up when the button is pressed. It will not be delivered to any government, organization, or person of authority. It especially will not result in 700 pages of anything.
